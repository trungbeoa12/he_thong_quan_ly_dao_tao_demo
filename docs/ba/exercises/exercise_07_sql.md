# Exercise 07 - SQL

## Context

Management wants training analytics for 2025 and 2026.

## Requirement

3. Calculate training cost by course.
4. Find courses with the most learners.
5. Calculate average training hours per employee.
6. Calculate training cost per employee.
7. Compare total training hours between 2025 and 2026.
8. Calculate PASS rate by course.
9. Find instructors with the highest teaching hours.
10. Calculate registrations by training method.
11. Calculate cost by cost type.
12. Find departments with no training participation.
13. Find employees absent from all sessions in a class.
14. Calculate completion rate by quarter.
15. Compare online and offline pass rates.
16. Find classes over capacity.
17. Find cancelled classes by month.
18. Calculate average score by instructor.
19. Find learners with failed results.
20. Calculate hotel and travel cost by region.
21. Find top 10 expensive classes.
22. Calculate material cost per learner.
23. Find classes without attendance records.
24. Find registered learners without results.
25. Calculate training hours by employee level.
26. Calculate training cost by salary group.
27. Calculate registrations by generation group.
28. Find duplicate registration attempts if audit data exists.
29. Compare planned hours and actual attended hours.
30. Calculate KPI summary by year.

Thirty SQL statements with explanation.
