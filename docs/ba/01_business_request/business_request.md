# Business Request

## Background

The organization currently manages training activities through Excel files, emails, and manual consolidation.

## Current Problems

- Training data is distributed across many files.
- Employee training history is difficult to trace.
- Training hours are hard to calculate accurately.
- Training cost consolidation is manual and slow.
- Instructor assignments are not centrally tracked.
- Reports require manual effort and are inconsistent.
- Data definitions differ between departments.

## Business Goal

Build a centralized Training Management System to manage courses, classes, schedules, registrations, attendance, results, costs, and reports.

## Expected Benefits

- Single source of truth for training data.
- Clear employee training history.
- Faster KPI and compliance reporting.
- Traceable cost calculation.
- Better support for BA training, SIT, UAT, and change request analysis.
